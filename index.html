Reflection

For my H1, I used clamp(28px, 4vw, 56px). I chose 28px as the minimum so the heading is never too small to read on a phone, 56px as the maximum so it does not look huge on a big desktop screen, and 4vw as the preferred value so it grows and shrinks smoothly with the screen width. The minimum and maximum are important because they set safe limits — the text can scale, but it never becomes unreadable or oversized. I tested the page by opening it in Chrome and slowly resizing the window from a narrow phone width up to a wide desktop width, watching the headings and body text scale smoothly and the cards change from one column to two and then three. Everything stayed readable at every size.
